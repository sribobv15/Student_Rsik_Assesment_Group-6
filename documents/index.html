Student Risk Assessment Algorithm

Identifying Students at Risk of Academic Failure Using Academic Data Variables

For this group project, we have prepared a prototype of an algorithm which aims to predict student performance based on multiple metrics found in this public dataset found on Kaggle.com.

This dataset contains 649 student records and 33 variables.

  • Study time
  • Absences
  • Final Grade

Preliminary correlation analysis points to the following three potential predictors:

  • Previous academic failures (`failures`): −0.393
  • Weekly study time (`studytime`):+0.250
  • School absences (`absences`):−0.091

Below is a general overview of the results:

Summary of Results:

Data quality: All 649 student records were processed successfully, with no missing values, invalid values, or duplicate

records detected by our cleaning checks.

Risk classification: The algorithm classified 367 students (56.5%) as Low Risk, 260 (40.1%) as Medium Risk, and 22 (3.4%) as

High Risk.

Prediction accuracy: The algorithm achieved an overall binary pass/fail prediction accuracy of 84.90% using the final grade (

G3) for evaluation.

Correctly identified failures: Of the 100 students who actually failed (G3 < 10), the algorithm correctly identified 12 as

High Risk.

Missed failures: The algorithm failed to identify 88 of the 100 students who actually failed, indicating that the current

risk-scoring rules need improvement.

False alarms: Ten students were classified as High Risk but actually passed, demonstrating that risk factors do not always

lead to academic failure.

Main conclusion: Although the algorithm achieved relatively high overall accuracy, it identified only 12% of students who

actually failed. This suggests that accuracy alone is not sufficient to evaluate its effectiveness.
